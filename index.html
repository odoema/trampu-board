What marketing should do next

Use the weakest stage of the funnel to choose the next message.

If delivery is weak

Fix audience quality, sender reputation and bounced contacts before increasing volume.

If clicks are weak

Test the subject, opening line, proof and CTA. Keep the step-by-step instructions short.

If sign-ups are weak

Reduce friction on the landing page, show social proof and make the first ride action obvious.
